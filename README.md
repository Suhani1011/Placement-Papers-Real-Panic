Placement Papers, Real Panic is an AI-powered voice-based mock interview simulator designed to help final-year students and fresh graduates prepare for campus placement interviews in a more realistic and interactive way. The project focuses on a common problem faced by students: although many candidates prepare technical questions by reading and writing answers, they get very few opportunities to actually practice speaking their answers aloud in an interview-like environment.

Our MVP allows users to enter their name, select an interview role, choose an interview language, and upload their resume. The system uses the candidate’s resume and selected role to generate personalized interview questions instead of relying only on generic questions. The interview consists of a balanced set of personal, resume-based, technical, practical, and behavioral questions.

The platform provides a voice-based interview experience where the AI interviewer speaks the question aloud. Users can listen to the question, repeat it using the speaker button when needed, and answer using their microphone. If a candidate gets stuck or is not satisfied with an answer, they can discard the recording and record the response again.

Speech-to-text converts the candidate’s spoken answer into text for further processing. The MVP supports English, Hindi, and Hinglish interaction while retaining commonly used technical terminology such as Python, SQL, C++, JavaScript, React, Power BI, Tableau, and other interview-related terms.

One of the key features of the project is the separation of content evaluation and delivery evaluation. AI-based content analysis focuses on relevance, technical correctness, completeness, clarity, strengths, and areas for improvement. Delivery analysis separately examines speaking speed in words per minute and filler-word usage. This helps students understand whether they need to improve their actual answer content, their communication delivery, or both.

The system also provides an English improvement version for Hindi and Hinglish responses, helping candidates understand how the same answer can be expressed naturally in professional English.

This project is developed as an MVP for CODEX 2026 under the Smart Education domain, with future scope including additional regional languages, advanced speech analysis, improved pronunciation and prosody analysis, and enhanced long-term performance tracking.
